02 / the drill

how the
bowl fills.

five steps. a small bot on a server does them on a timer. the code is public, so you can read exactly what it does.

  1. claim the treats.

    the keeper claims $STAY's creator fees into a wallet only it uses.

  2. buy a slice.

    part of the sol buys $STAY on the pumpswap pool.

  3. pair it.

    the $STAY it bought goes with the sol it didn't spend.

  4. pour it in.

    both go into the $STAY/sol pool as liquidity.

  5. burn the receipt.

    the lp tokens from that add are burned. burned lp can't be redeemed, so nobody can take that liquidity out. us included.

the buried bone.

the keeper keeps 4% of supply in its wallet as a reserve. it never sells it and never pours it in. if the reserve drops under 4%, a quarter of each batch buys back toward it. at 4%, everything goes in the bowl.

the small print, big.

network fees come out first. then the bone, if it needs topping up. then the slice that buys. then the pour. no trades means no fees, and no fees means nothing to pour. if the keeper is down, nothing gets poured either. other people's lp is theirs, not ours.
