who stay is.

page 01

stay is a mutt. tan coat, white bib, floppy brown ears, one brown patch over his left eye. if anyone asks the breed, the answer is "very good."

he wears a red collar with a brass tag. the tag says what he is.

he sits. that's the pose. he doesn't run, jump, fetch or roll over in the main art. the meme maker is allowed to give him a side eye or a bad night's sleep. that's as far as it goes.

stay is his name, and it's also the only thing he does.

2

why staying.

page 02

every day, something gets shouted at you. sell. buy. panic. fetch the bottom. a kol on a space says come here. your landlord says heel. monday says get off the couch.

most people try to obey all of it. they chase every stick that gets thrown. it doesn't work, and it's tiring.

stay went to obedience school and only learned one command. so when anything gets shouted at him, he does the one thing he knows.

it works on more than charts. try it on your group chat.

it's a joke about a dog. it says nothing about the price, and "stay" is not advice to hold anything.

3

the command.

page 03

the sit: bum down, back straight, paws together, looking straight at the camera like nothing is happening.

  1. find a seat that isn't moving. a chair, a step, a patch of grass.
  2. sit down. slowly. whatever it is, it's already been shouted.
  3. paws together. hands on knees works.
  4. look at the camera. blank face.
  5. hold it. don't move until it stops.
  6. post it with #staying.

not on roads, ledges, train platforms or anything with wheels. if a real thing is actually coming at you, move. the sit is for candles and group chats.

4

the bowl.

page 04

the creator fees don't go to the dev. they go in the bowl, which is the $STAY/sol pool on pumpswap. a small bot called the keeper does this on a timer:

  1. claim. it claims the creator fees into its own wallet.
  2. buy. part of the sol buys $STAY on the pool.
  3. pair. it pairs that $STAY with the sol it didn't spend.
  4. pour. both go into the pool as liquidity.
  5. burn. it burns the lp tokens it got back. burned lp can't be redeemed, so nobody can take that liquidity out, including us.

the buried bone.

the keeper holds 4% of supply (40m $STAY) as a reserve. it never sells it and never pours it in. below 4%, a quarter of each batch buys back toward it. at 4%, all of each batch goes in the bowl.

the small print.

  • network fees and running costs come out first, then the bone top-up, then the buy, then the pour.
  • it only works in batches above a small threshold, and keeps a little sol back for fees.
  • no trades means no fees means nothing to pour. if the keeper is down, nothing is poured.
  • we burn the lp from our adds. other people's lp is theirs.
  • a deeper pool can mean less slippage. it is not a price floor and it doesn't stop losses.

the keeper's code is public. see chapter 8. every add is listed on the bowl page with its solscan link.

7

faq.

page 07
is this a copy of another dog coin?

no. it's an original dog with one original command. lots of coins have dogs. this one sits.

does the dev get the creator fees?

no. they go to the keeper's wallet and from there into the pool, with the lp burned. it's all on-chain and the keeper's code is on github.

can the liquidity be pulled?

not the liquidity the keeper adds. its lp is burned in the same run. other people's lp is theirs to do what they like with.

what's the 4% reserve for?

it's the buried bone: a reserve that never gets sold or poured. it's there so the project always has a stake in itself.

does the bowl mean the price can't go down?

no. a deeper pool can mean less slippage on trades. it isn't a floor. prices go down. he stays anyway.

what does "stay" mean?

it's a greeting and a joke about a dog. it isn't advice to buy, hold or sell anything.

someone dmed me about a stay airdrop.

it's a scam. there's no airdrop and nobody from here will ever dm you first or ask for a seed phrase.
