the bowl · where the fees go

fees go
in the bowl.

every batch, a small bot (the keeper) claims the creator fees, buys a bit of $STAY, pairs it with sol, pours both into the pool and burns the receipt. nobody can pull that liquidity back out. not us, not anyone.

  1. claim

    creator fees land in the keeper's wallet.

  2. buy

    a slice of the sol buys $STAY on the pool.

  3. pair

    the $STAY it bought + the sol it didn't spend.

  4. pour

    both go into the $STAY/sol pumpswap pool.

  5. burn

    the lp from that add is burned. it can't come back out.

see the bowl → read the keeper's code → a deeper pool can mean less slippage. it is not a price floor.
